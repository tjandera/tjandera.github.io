Inde Navarrette Fan page

Introduction

Hey everyone, it’s Inde! First off, I just want to say a massive thank you for all the love on Obsession—playing Nikki and getting to go completely off the rails on screen was an absolute dream, and seeing how much you guys embraced the horror chaos has been totally surreal. I’m so excited to be here and hang out with you all, whether you’ve been along for the ride for a while or you just survived watching the movie!

 

Few Interesting Facts About me!

  • Every uncanny facial expression, unsettling smile, and vocal shift you saw me pull off as Nikki in Obsession was 100% real—no CGI or AI was used on my face.
  • For that intense car scene in Obsession, I executed the stunt myself in a single take while wearing a protective helmet hidden under my wig.
  • Prior to diving into horror for Obsession, I played Estela de la Cruz in Netflix's 13 Reasons Why and Sarah Cortez in Superman & Lois.
